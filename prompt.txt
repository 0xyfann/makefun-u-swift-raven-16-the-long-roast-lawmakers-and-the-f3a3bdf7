Write ONE original satirical sentence, at most 320 characters, roasting a
lawmaker who regulates AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a
file to PDF. The sentence must:

- Set up the lawmaker's authority (writing regulation) and then undercut
  it with the file they cannot convert — a real turn, not just an insult.
- Keep its aim on lawmakers and the rules they write (power and
  competence), not random abuse.
- Use one specific, unexpected detail (e.g. a filename, a habit) instead
  of a worn tech-illiteracy cliche.
- Read clean in one pass, stand alone, and land the punch on the last
  few words.

Deliverable: a Python program (stdlib only) that writes exactly that
sentence to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (default /output/entry.txt),
with an internal check that the text is 1–320 characters and a single
sentence.
