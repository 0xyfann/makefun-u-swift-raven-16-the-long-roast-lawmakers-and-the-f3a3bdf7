One satirical sentence roasting a lawmaker who writes 900 pages of AI regulation yet still emails his intern a Word file to turn into a PDF, for the make.fun competition "The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the ...".

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes a single original line to
`entry.txt` in the directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).

The line:

> The senator who just wrote 900 pages of AI regulation still emails his intern a Word file named FINAL_final_v7_REAL.docx asking her to turn it into a PDF, then wonders why the future keeps sending him to voicemail.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
